# Habitloop — `goal.md` (the why)  · EXAMPLE

> The north star. Everything else serves this. Keep to ~1 page.

## Problem
People start habits but quit within ~2 weeks. Today's trackers are cluttered and
over-gamified, so the daily check-in feels like a chore and people stop opening them.

## What Habitloop is
A dead-simple, calm habit tracker. One tap to check in; a streak to keep you going.

## Who it's for
Individuals (25–40) building 1–5 personal daily habits on iPhone. They value speed and
calm over configuration and features.

## Core scenario
Maya opens Habitloop at breakfast, taps two habits done, snoozes one, and sees her
12-day reading streak. A gentle evening reminder catches the last habit.

## Definition of done (for the product)
A new user can, in under 2 minutes: create a habit, check in for today, see a streak of
1, and receive a reminder tomorrow — all offline.

## Success metrics
- **Day-7 retention ≥ 30%.**
- **≥ 40% of active users hold a 7+ day streak** on at least one habit.
- **Daily check-in rate ≥ 0.6** per active user.

## Non-goals (v1)
- ❌ Social / sharing / leaderboards
- ❌ Points, badges, heavy gamification
- ❌ Android, web, watch, widgets
- ❌ AI coaching, weekly/custom schedules
